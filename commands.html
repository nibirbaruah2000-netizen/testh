Commands

All 75 commands

Type /help in any server to see the list the bot is running.

Moderation

Core moderation with case logging. Requires the matching Discord permission or the mod role.

/kickKick a member from the server.
/banBan a user from the server.
/unbanUnban a user by their ID.
/timeoutTemporarily stop a member from chatting.
/untimeoutRemove a member's timeout.
/warnWarn a member. Thresholds may trigger automatic action.
/warningsList a member's active warnings.
/clearwarningsClear all active warnings for a member.
/purgeBulk delete recent messages in this channel.
/slowmodeSet the slowmode delay for this channel.
/lockStop everyone from sending messages in this channel.
/unlockAllow messages again in this channel.
/caseShow a moderation case by number.
/casesList recent moderation cases for a member.

Moderation extras

Nicknames, warning removal and softbans.

/nickChange or reset a member's nickname.
/removewarnRemove one specific warning by its ID.
/softbanBan and immediately unban a member to clear their recent messages.

Server configuration

Requires the Manage Server permission.

/config showShow this server's current settings.
/config modroleSet the role that can use moderation commands.
/config logchannelSet where moderation cases are posted.
/config thresholdsSet how many warnings trigger automatic punishments.

AntiNuke

Protection against mass destructive actions. Requires Manage Server.

/antinuke statusShow AntiNuke settings.
/antinuke toggleTurn AntiNuke on or off.
/antinuke thresholdSet how many actions trigger AntiNuke, and within how long.
/antinuke punishmentChoose strip (remove dangerous roles) or ban.
/antinuke allowExempt a user from AntiNuke.
/antinuke disallowRemove a user's AntiNuke exemption.
/antinuke allowlistList users exempt from AntiNuke.

Economy

Earn, spend and move coins. Balances are shared across every server.

/balanceCheck your coins and bank balance.
/dailyClaim your daily coins.
/weeklyClaim your weekly coins.
/workWork a job to earn coins. Cooldown: 1 hour.
/paySend coins to another member.
/depositMove coins from your wallet into the bank.
/withdrawMove coins from the bank into your wallet.
/robTry to steal coins from a member. Risky. Cooldown: 1 hour.
/leaderboardShow the richest users across all servers.
/profileShow a user's economy profile.
/setbioSet the bio shown on your profile.
/repGive a member a reputation point. Once per day.

Shop and trading

Buy, sell and trade items. Any server's shop works from any server.

/shopBrowse a server's shop.
/buyBuy an item from any server's shop by its ID.
/sellSell an item back for half its shop price.
/inventorySee the items you own.
/tradeOffer an item to another member, for coins or free.
/eco giveGive coins to a user. Requires Administrator.
/eco takeTake coins from a user. Requires Administrator.
/shopadmin additemAdd an item to this server's shop.
/shopadmin removeitemRemove an item from this server's shop.
