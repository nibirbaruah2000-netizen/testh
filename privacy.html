Privacy

Privacy policy

Last updated: 19 October 2026

What Fourthnight stores

Fourthnight stores only what it needs to run its features:

  • Discord user and server IDs, for economy balances, warnings, moderation cases, and per-server settings.
  • Economy data: coins, bank balance, reputation, bio, inventory, and cooldown timestamps.
  • Moderation data: warnings, case history, and the reasons you give for actions.
  • AntiNuke settings and allowlist entries for each server.

What it does not store

Fourthnight does not read or store the content of your messages. It doesn't use the Message Content intent.

Who can see your data

Each server's moderation data is only visible to that server's staff through bot commands. Economy balances are visible to other users only through commands such as /balance, /profile and /leaderboard.

Deleting your data

To request deletion of your data, contact the bot operator through the GitHub repository.

Self-hosted copies

If you run your own copy of Fourthnight, you are the data controller for that copy and are responsible for how it stores and protects data.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the GitHub repository at github.com/NimbleFlea8613.
