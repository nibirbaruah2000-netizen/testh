Features

Built for safe, active communities

Each system works on its own, and together they cover protection, moderation and engagement.

🛡️

AntiNuke

Watches channel and role deletes, bans, kicks and webhook creation. Triggers when one user exceeds your limit in a set time window. Response: strip dangerous roles or ban. Off by default; each server opts in.

⚖️

Moderation with case logs

Every action gets a numbered case (Case #1, #2, …) posted to your log channel. Warnings trigger automatic timeouts, kicks or bans at thresholds you set.

🔒

Safe permissions

Moderators can't act on the owner, the bot, or anyone with a role at or above their own. Commands accept either a Discord permission or your configured mod role.

🌐

Inter-server economy

Your coins, bank and inventory follow you to every server with Fourthnight. Pay friends in other communities and buy from any shop by its item ID.

🤝

Trading

Offer items for coins or as gifts. The recipient confirms with a button, and the swap either completes fully or not at all.

🎰

Games

Slots with bets, dice of any size, coin flips, rock-paper-scissors, 8-ball and a choose-for-me picker.

📊

Polls and reminders

Three-option polls with reaction voting, plus one-off reminders delivered to the channel where they were set.

⚙️

Per-server settings

Mod role, log channel, warning thresholds and AntiNuke limits are stored separately for each server.
